1. Who operates the application

TikTok Scheduler is a desktop application operated by its individual developer. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or Google. Privacy questions can be sent to [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

2. Information the application stores

The application stores its TikTok Client Key and Client Secret in a local .env file. It stores the TikTok access and refresh tokens in state/tokens.json, and the local publishing queue and TikTok publish identifiers in state/schedule.json. These files are stored on the user's computer; the application does not send them to a separate server operated by its developer.

3. Videos and captions

Video and music files are processed locally on the user's computer. When the user starts a post, the prepared video and its caption are transmitted directly to TikTok through TikTok's Content Posting API. TikTok processes that information under TikTok's own terms and privacy policy. The application requests that posts be visible to “Only you.”

4. Login and Google

Account authorization takes place on TikTok's website in the user's browser. If the user chooses to sign in to TikTok with Google, that sign-in is handled by TikTok and Google under their respective terms and privacy policies. TikTok Scheduler does not receive or store the user's Google password.

5. Analytics and cookies

The desktop application does not include its own analytics or advertising trackers. TikTok or Google may use cookies or similar technologies on their own websites during login and authorization.

6. Retention and deletion

Local credentials, tokens, and queue records remain on the user's computer until the user deletes the corresponding .env file or state folder. The user can revoke the application's access through TikTok account settings. Content already uploaded to TikTok is managed through TikTok.
